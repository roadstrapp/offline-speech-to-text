Two new speech models in Speech models. Canary 180m flash is a 139 MB download that transcribed English and German about twice as fast as Parakeet v3 in our phone tests, with slightly more mistakes, mostly in German. It is now the suggestion for Speed and size. It needs one chosen spoken language, or the language detector if you speak several. Parakeet ultra made fewer mistakes than Parakeet v3, especially in German, at about the same speed.
