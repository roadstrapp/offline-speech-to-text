Updated the speech engine to transcribe.cpp 0.4.0. Audio files now decode in bounded blocks, with overlapping transcription windows and recoverable partial results. Dictation, file transcription and subtitles share one model, with priority for waiting voice input. Improved speech detection, silence trimming, keyboard switching, accessibility and Android 16 compatibility. Reduced native debug data in the APK. Existing dictionaries, transcript recovery and downloaded models remain supported.
